Episode Description

In this episode we talk with Leo Wilhelmi, fourth-generation owner of the Buchinger Wilhelmi fasting clinics in Germany, Spain and soon France. Just as his great-grandfather Dr. Otto Buchinger transformed his incurable illness into building the famous Buchinger fasting method, Leo turned his own period of challenge into the vision for a new clinic in southern France. A pattern that he is also observing with many of his patients as he shares their stories of how connecting back to one’s true self can be the beginning of building something extraordinary.
