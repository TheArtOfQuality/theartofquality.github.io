Bio

Alex Carabi is a coach and facilitator. He lives in the north of Sweden where he runs a retreat center and farm with his family. His work is devoted to bringing heart and soul to business, leadership, and community. Through family and systemic constellations his work attunes to systemic fields across generations in families and organizations. He is the author of On Relationality: Lessons from Martin Buber on Living in Relation with Self, Others, the World, and Life Itself.

Episode Description

In this conversation, William and Alex explore family constellations and the hidden dynamics that shape our lives, relationships, and organizations. They discuss what it means to trust life as it unfolds - to meet difficulty as an opportunity to learn and grow, surrender the need for control, and, as Alex puts it, take ourselves less seriously while taking life more seriously.
