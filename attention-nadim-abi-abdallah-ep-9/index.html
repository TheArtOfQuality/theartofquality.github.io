Episode Description

In this episode of The Art of Quality in Attention, William sits down with his friend and former colleague Nadim Abi Abdallah to explore the practice of fieldwork as a core component of the investment research process.

The conversation focuses on how Nadim approaches fieldwork and what quality looks like in the fieldwork process — drawing on years spent in direct conversations with operators, building a ground-level understanding of businesses, developing deeper understanding of companies through firsthand insight. They discuss what it means to conduct fieldwork in an effective, responsible, and professional way, and why this method remains underutilized despite its potential.

The episode offers a practical look into the mindset, discipline, and curiosity required to do this work well, and aims to provide valuable tools for investors seeking to deepen their investment research process.
