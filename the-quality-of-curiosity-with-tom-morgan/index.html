Episode Description

A wide-ranging conversation on curiosity as a discipline. Tom defines wisdom as the ability to sense and align with “attractors” — the subtle forces that guide coherence and quality in life — and explains how curiosity enhances results by keeping us open to faint signals, enabling flexible pivots, and building a reservoir of experience for intuitive, informed decisions.

They explore the power of storytelling and myth as maps that reveal deeper truths, embodied intuition and the “butterfly feeling” that accompanies an intuitive nudge, the balance between logic and intuition, and why conversations about meaning and connection matter in business.
