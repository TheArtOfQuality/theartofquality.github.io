Episode Description

In this episode, John Candeto sits down with Andrew Farah, the Founder and CEO of Density. Andrew and his team are at the forefront of using AI to improve how we build and create.

They explore how using AI is like encountering a new species, why this is the best time in history to be a creative person, why the future will be owned by people who build tools, and how AI can amplify human ambition.
