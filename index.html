Our shows

The Art of Quality in General

Essays and wide-ranging conversations that explore quality beyond any single domain - culture, nature, craft, and the everyday practice of paying attention.

→

The Art of Quality in Investing

with John Candeto

Conversations with investors and operators who compound quality over decades - exploring what it means to find, build, and hold excellence in the market.

→

The Art of Quality in Attention

with William Oliver

Exploring the inner dimensions of quality - how we perceive, attend to, and ultimately cultivate excellence through deeper presence and awareness.

→

The Art of Quality in Systems

with Paul Higgins

What patterns from nature and complex systems can teach us about building businesses that endure - integrating systemic thinking with long-term value creation.

→

The Art of Quality in Building

with Ferdinand Bennigsen

The craft of building organizations, products and lives with lasting quality - conversations with founders and operators who build with conviction and care.

→

Our Hosts

Practitioners, asking practitioners.


John Candeto

Host - The Art of Quality in Investing

Advisor → Builder → Investor. Seeking wisdom and following curiosity. Building things as I find opportunities to serve others well. Founder and Managing Partner of the Phronesis Fund. Co-Founder of Workforce Data Alliance.


William Oliver

Host - The Art of Quality in Attention

Will is the co-founder of In Practise, a primary research firm serving the needs of long-term investors and operators of quality companies.


Paul Higgins

Host - The Art of Quality in Systems

Paul spent a decade driving acquisitions, investing and operating businesses across Europe for EY, Accenture and later in private equity before going out on his own in 2021 to build a new generation of businesses through Invrt.


Ferdinand Bennigsen

Host - The Art of Quality in Building

Co-owner of the asset management firms Carl von Rohrer and ATACAMA Partners based in Munich, that grow their clients’ assets by uncovering structural changes and making high-conviction investments in the public markets.
