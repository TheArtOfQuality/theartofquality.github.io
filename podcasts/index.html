About the Series

Truth is a correct understanding of how the world really works. Wisdom is aligning one’s actions to truth. The Art of Quality in Investing is an open ended investigation of how outstanding investors think, decide, and act. The Art of Quality in Systems with Paul Higgins

Presented by INVRT

About the Series

The Art of Quality in Systems explores the invisible patterns that allow systems—businesses, communities, ecosystems, and family enterprises – to endure and evolve. Beyond metrics, we look at the felt qualities that make some systems resilient and regenerative while others grow brittle and decay. Through conversations with master practitioners, we uncover what it takes to build something that lasts for decades – or even centuries – in a world ignoring ancient wisdom and fixated on speed and optimization.

About the Host

Paul Higgins brings over a decade of M&A and investment experience helping founders build global enterprises designed for multi-generational success. As host of The Art of Quality in Systems, Paul explores the patterns that distinguish enduring excellence from temporary performance, drawing on his work with quality-focused businesses across diverse industries and his deep practice in systems thinking. See invrt.io for further details.
