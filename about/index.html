About The Art of Quality

What it is

The Art of Quality is a podcast and a growing community about quality as it is actually lived. In long-form conversations, our hosts sit down with practitioners who have spent years, often decades, mastering their craft: investors, operators, builders, artists, musicians and wisdom teachers. We want to understand what quality looks and feels like up close, and how it compounds over time.

We aren't here to study quality from a distance. We're practitioners too, looking for true stories that help us orient our own work and lives, and sharing what we learn along the way. Each conversation is a chance to come away seeing a little more clearly and knowing a little better what to do.

Meet the team
Our mission

To give people seeking mastery across disciplines a home where they can meet, learn from each other and bring quality to life in their work.

We believe quality is a right relationship with context: care for the people, the craft and the generations that come before and after us. We don't claim to have mastered it. We're learning in public, and every conversation, gathering and publication is part of that practice.
