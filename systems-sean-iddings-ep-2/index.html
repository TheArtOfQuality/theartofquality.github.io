Episode Description

Paul Higgins sits down with investor, builder and author Sean Iddings to explore how music, nature and business can teach us to perceive quality differently. Drawing on decades of studying enduring businesses and exceptional operators, Sean examines the patterns that rarely show up in the numbers - from incentives and culture to discipline, relationships and the ability to see what others miss.
